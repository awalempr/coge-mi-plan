# Problem discovery: voice memo from a former hospital nurse (Puerto Rico)

The project started from a 4-minute Spanish voice memo by a former hospital nurse in Puerto Rico. To protect the people mentioned in it, the raw recording and transcript are not published. This is an anonymized summary.

## What the memo describes (translated, paraphrased)
1. **Specialists who don't take your plan.** "There's no real data. You have to call and call and call to find out who takes your plan." The speaker called many dermatologists, and none accepted their plan. The cause they name is low, late payments from health plans, which lead specialists to stop accepting them.
2. **Nurse workload.** Nurses used to carry 8–10 patients each and track almost everything by hand, so things got missed. A digital per-patient task list would help.
3. **Archaic lab lines.** At one large private hospital network's labs, patients take a number and lose most of a day for a blood test, so many pay a deductible elsewhere instead.

## Why we chose problem 1
| | Patient registry (chosen) | Nurse task tool | Lab virtual line |
|---|---|---|---|
| Who it helps | Every resident with a plan (3.2M people; 1.4M on Plan Vital; 771K aged 65+) | Nurses in one hospital per sale | Patients of one lab network |
| Time to launch | Weeks, on WhatsApp | Hospital procurement + EHR integration | One operator partnership |
| Who pays | Health plans (federal 90-day directory rule) | Hospital IT budget | The lab operator |
| Patient records needed | None | Full records | Scheduling data |

Problems 2 and 3 are on the roadmap for the same platform.
