# 2-minute demo video script

The video is a screen recording of the live app (https://coge-mi-plan.netlify.app) with your voice over it. The middle 49 seconds run on the **▶ Demo** button. Its captions appear on screen at the times below, so each narration line is written to land with its caption. Speak at a calm pace. You don't need to fill every second.

## Before you record (5 minutes)
1. Chrome, one window, 1440×900 or larger, browser zoom at 100%. Close other tabs and silence notifications (Focus mode).
2. Open the site, type any message once and wait for the answer. This wakes up the AI server. Then **refresh the page** so the numbers start fresh (14 listed / 1 confirmed / 8 ghosts).
3. Click **EN** (top right) so the captions match your English narration.
4. Record with **Cmd + Shift + 5 → Record Selected Portion**, around the browser window, with your microphone selected under Options.
5. Do one silent dry run of the Demo button so you know the rhythm.

## If something goes wrong while recording
- **The AI answer takes a while or shows "Local parser":** keep talking. The app still answers, using the offline parser. If the take matters, refresh and redo it after the server is warm.
- **You stumble over a line:** keep going. One natural take beats a perfect one, and judges care about the product.
- **The recording runs past 2:00:** trim the end in QuickTime (Edit → Trim).

## Uploading
Export as 1080p MP4, upload to YouTube as **Unlisted** (or to Vimeo), and paste the link into the DevPost "Video demo link" field.
