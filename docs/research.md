# Research facts (from web search summaries; verify exact wording before quoting)

1. PR physicians fell from ~14,000 (2006) to ~9,000 (2016). https://www.pbs.org/newshour/show/sick-puerto-ricans-are-facing-long-waits-to-see-the-doctor
2. 8,000+ physicians stopped practicing in PR over 13 years; waits up to 9 months for some specialists (2023). https://grupocne.org/2023/03/05/exodus-of-doctors-a-problem-that-defies-simple-solutions/
3. Colegio de Médicos president (Sept 2025): plan rates frozen 20–30 years, payment delays; 30+ municipalities without a psychiatrist. https://www.metro.pr/noticias/2025/09/21/presidente-del-colegio-medico-confirma-cierres-de-hospitales-en-el-horizonte-y-alerta-sobre-falta-de-especialistas-en-puerto-rico
4. 95% of PR Medicare beneficiaries are in Medicare Advantage (2024), highest in the US. https://www.ajmc.com/view/medicare-advantage-hits-32-8m-enrollees-54-of-spending
5. ~1.44M people on Medicaid / Plan Vital (Mar 2024; other sources say 1.24–1.3M). ASES Quality Strategy Evaluation, June 2024.
6. CMS review: 52.2% of MA directory locations had at least one inaccuracy (round 2); ~49% in round 3. https://www.hhs.gov/guidance/sites/default/files/hhs-guidance-documents/CMS/OY1-Provider-Directory-Review-Industry-Report_Final_508-compliant_Revised.pdf
7. Senate Finance secret-shopper study (May 2023): only 18% of calls to listed MA mental-health providers produced an appointment; 33% of listings were inaccurate or unreachable. https://www.finance.senate.gov/imo/media/doc/050323%20Ghost%20Network%20Hearing%20-%20Secret%20Shopper%20Study%20Report.pdf
8. No Surprises Act §116 (42 U.S.C. §300gg-115): verify directory data at least every 90 days, update within 2 business days, answer patient directory inquiries within 1 business day.
9. CPI (Apr 2026): of 40 specialists/dentists sampled, half did not accept any First Medical coverage; half of those that did refused the Plan Vital version. https://www.metro.pr/noticias/2026/04/23/first-medical-health-plan-mantiene-por-anos-el-impago-a-proveedores-y-reduce-servicios-a-pacientes/
10. 25% of orthopedists did not accept Plan Vital due to non-payment and low rates (Noticel, 2019). https://noticel.com/ultima-hora/20190709/arrinconados-y-subvalorados-los-ortopedas-en-puerto-rico/
11. 96% of PR residents have a smartphone; 91% use the internet daily (2023). https://newsismybusiness.com/internet-usage-in-puerto-rico-increases-to-91
12. 24% of PR residents are 65+ (770,861 people; ACS 2023). https://newsismybusiness.com/census-puerto-ricos-65-population-doubles-15-and-under-group
